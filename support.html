AIQBase Support

Help for installation, learning progress, purchases, and data management.

Learning progress

The current version stores the learning profile locally on the device. If you clear app data or uninstall the app, local progress may be lost unless you exported a backup.

Purchases

For a mobile purchase, first check the purchase history of the store where AIQBase was installed. Billing and refund requests are handled under that store's rules.

Restore access

When store billing is enabled, paid access is restored based on the purchase status supplied by the store. Use the same store account that made the original purchase.

Privacy and data

Read our Privacy Policy. Local learning data can be removed using the app's data-management controls or by clearing app storage.

Developer contact

The official support email and developer contact details are provided on the AIQBase listing in Google Play and the App Store. This page will be updated if a dedicated support mailbox is introduced.
