Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AIQBase handles information when you use the AIQBase website and mobile application.

1. Learning data

In the current version, learning progress such as answered questions, mistakes, repetition history, favorites, and learning statistics is stored locally on the user's device. AIQBase does not require an account for this local learning profile.

2. Information we do not collect by default

AIQBase does not, by default, upload the local learning profile to an AIQBase server. The current app does not require access to contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs, or health records for its core learning functionality.

3. Purchases

Paid digital features in the mobile application may be offered through Google Play or Apple's App Store. Purchase processing is handled by the applicable app store. AIQBase does not receive or store your full payment-card number. The app may receive purchase status or entitlement information from the store so that paid features can be enabled.

4. Website and infrastructure

The AIQBase website is delivered through third-party hosting and network infrastructure. Those providers may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, device/browser information, and security logs as necessary to deliver and protect the website.

5. Local storage

The website and app may use browser or device storage to preserve learning progress and preferences. Clearing application data, browser storage, or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored progress unless a backup has been exported by the user.

6. Children's privacy

AIQBase is an educational product. We do not knowingly design the current service to collect personal information from children. If the product is later offered to schools or institutions with accounts or cloud synchronization, the privacy notice will be updated before those features are enabled.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers only when necessary to operate the website, distribute the mobile app, process purchases, maintain security, or comply with law.

8. Data deletion

Because the current learning profile is stored locally, users can remove it from within the app's data-management controls or by clearing application data. If cloud accounts are introduced later, this policy will be updated with server-side deletion instructions.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the service. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AIQBase develops. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the current version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information shown on the official AIQBase Google Play or App Store listing, or visit our Support page.
